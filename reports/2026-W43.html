🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

Message search goes global, legacy competition storage retired, desktop syncs with frontend search

The headline feature this week is a full message search system. GelatoGenesis shipped a permission-aware backend that lets users search across all waves they have access to, with author and wave filters, and ragnep wired up the frontend with global search integration. Previously, finding a specific post meant scrolling through waves manually. Now you can search by text, author, or wave. prxt6529 also synced the desktop app's renderer with the new frontend search and collection updates, so the desktop experience stays parity with the web.

Frontend: search, profiles, and mobile polish

53 frontend PRs merged this week, the busiest repo. ragnep added a private Following list to your own profile, refined the listings and purchase review layout, made Meme card collectors easier to read on phones, and fixed collector tooltip dismissal and focused filter visibility. GelatoGenesis restored Main Stage submission headers across Chat and Leaderboard views, kept cached wave chat immediate across competition tabs, and restored fast My Votes cards and responsive Chat tab loading. prxt6529 improved homepage artist and collaborator spacing, polished Safe Collecting guides with translated locales, and fixed the mobile lab production trigger and target concurrency.

Backend: legacy retirement and image validation

27 backend PRs merged. GelatoGenesis completed the retirement of legacy competition storage and infrastructure, a significant cleanup that removes old systems still consuming resources. He also added permission-aware message search across waves, restored fast My Votes cards and personal vote ordering, and notified voters when competition voting credits are returned. prxt6529 contributed several infrastructure hardening PRs: validating image formats before publishing drop and wave uploads, decoding HEIC resize sources with cached permanent rejections, reducing NFT worker contention noise, and monitoring background Lambda contention by processing impact.

6529-core: desktop keeps pace

13 PRs in the desktop app. prxt6529 synced the desktop renderer with frontend advanced search and collection updates, and removed tracked Kubo binaries to make CI downloads credential-free. punk6529 bundled Kubo 0.43.1, fetched and verified from one place, holding for its own release. The desktop app continues to track the frontend closely, with search and collection features now matching.

Team activity

All four team members were active this week. GelatoGenesis led with 44 PRs across frontend and backend, focused on the message search system and legacy retirement. prxt6529 contributed 21 PRs spanning desktop, backend image handling, and frontend polish. ragnep merged 20 PRs, mostly frontend UI refinements for profiles, listings, and mobile. punk6529 merged 8 PRs, concentrated on the desktop Kubo bundle and core infrastructure. About 150 dev wave drops this week across deploy and PR feed subwaves.

🍺 Socials

Marfa takes over the bar, punk6529 talks decentralization and Ministry of Finance, tito launches El Memeño

The week belonged to Marfa. Community members returning from the Art Blocks gathering in Marfa, Texas flooded the dive bar with stories, reflections, and the particular energy that comes from meeting your online friends in person. @DeltaSauce posted simply "Marfa was insane." @TarainAbstract shared a longer reflection from the airport: "At the airport after an amazing time in Marfa. @[maybe] we all kinda knew this but you are a gem. So so glad we got to connect." @maybe, who had been at the center of the gathering, posted about waking up at 4:30 AM Marfa time and not being able to get back to sleep. The Marfa bar project, funded by @Gray's Burn Mechanism card despite the wallet drain, was clearly a meaningful moment for everyone who attended.

punk6529 on decentralization, trolls, and the Ministry of Finance

punk6529 spent Oct 24 in the bar and Announcements wave thinking out loud about the road to decentralization. He posted two major Announcements threads. The first, "Squiggle 6529," congratulated the community for organizing the Chromie Squiggle acquisition without his involvement, calling it "a full step up from mint a card" and urging the network to take on increasingly complex initiatives. The second, "Ministry of Finance," laid out the economics: running 6529 costs a couple million dollars per year, and when the network goes decentralized it will need to finance those expenses and ideally much larger ones. He sketched a plan to scale from $2M to $5M to $10M to $100M per year while keeping the culture lean.

In the bar itself, he pushed back on the idea that minor philosophical differences are keeping people from contributing: "I actually do not think there are a ton of people who are on the sidelines dying to do things but are not because of some relatively minor philosophical differences." He also addressed a new troll with characteristic directness: "Don't feed the trolls. Figure out where you are going to run validator/s instead." And he framed the trolling as a sign of progress: "A good sign that we are making it is when the European Central Bank is trolling us!"

tito launches El Memeño competition

@tito announced the El Memeño Meme Competition, offering a chance at a Meme card selection plus climate change solutions. gpebbles, Ruskin, and Mada all committed to blast the contest winner with TDH on the Main Stage. tito framed it around the coming "Godzilla El Niño" and asked the bar for help bringing in more artists. @blake69 suggested the bar frens could vote and join with a meme on El Memeño. The competition is still open for new artist submissions.

Collaborator invitations go live for Main Stage

prxt0 announced a new feature on Oct 21: artists can now invite up to 5 collaborating artists when submitting work to The Memes Main Stage, or add them to an existing active submission. Collaborators appear publicly once they accept the invitation. This opens the door for more joint submissions and recognition of co-creators.
