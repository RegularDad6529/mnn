🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

punk6529 launches 6529-works repo with v1 contract suite, competitions migration completes

The biggest dev story this week was punk6529 opening a new repository called 6529-works, containing what appears to be the next generation of 6529 contract infrastructure. Roughly 30 PRs landed in the repo on Oct 11 alone, covering the full v1 architecture: content store, statement log, AccessManager governance, fixed-price and Dutch sale adapters, English auction adapter, VRF entropy module, artist registry, package registry with EIP-712 curation verifier, EconomicsResolver for per-product pricing, split wallets with currency allowlist and escrow fallback, and genesis configuration wiring. An ADR (Architecture Decision Record) was accepted the same day to rescope 6529 Stream as the network-curated 1/1 contract. This is a foundational build, not a feature patch.

Competition migration and platform improvements

GelatoGenesis completed migrating competitions to the new model across both staging and production, with some waves still processing. The migration included preserving completed wave history, restoring historical vote activity, and fixing migration comparison freshness for large waves. GelatoGenesis also fixed an intermittent NFT activity timeout that was breaking staging and production e2e tests, restored authenticated wave typing indicators, and added a frontend polish judge standard. simo6529 shipped a simpler Chat Wave creation flow with a visible creation entry and two screens, made wallet consolidation easier to find from the profile, added homepage engagement tracking reaching Mixpanel with a dashboard, and confirmed wave usage tracking. simo6529 was sick most of the week but kept prompting tasks from phone. prxt6529 fixed E2E readiness and dependency installation, bounded subscription cache eviction and recovered failed invalidations, restored authenticated wave typing indicators, and showed voting data on completed submissions with explanations for missing winner history. ragnep made three iOS PDF improvements: every PDF attachment page is now readable on iOS, zoom position is preserved, and memory usage is reduced for long PDFs. ragnep also added homepage engagement tracking, improved network group and rep/NIC forms, and made profile collections easier to browse.

Community tech feedback

tito reported that on iOS, only the first page of a PDF loads in the preview, which ragnep subsequently fixed across three PRs. gpebbles flagged that clicking waves from the joined list routes to the competitions tab, and wave navigation felt less smooth with everything reloading. GelatoGenesis noted they were away from their computer but would investigate. Cuttle reported not receiving DM notifications when new DMs arrive. Bicasso found a bug when trying to video record a submission for X. boostgrind asked about adding multiple profiles for collaborations on submissions, and noted that on mobile with MetaMask, the subscriptions tab is not visible. Zoku reported that emoji memory is not being saved. ikertje raised that links are prohibited in Main Stage chat, and teexels (who has admin rights) accidentally posted a link and self-deleted it with humor: "If I said that I was testing something nobody will believe me!"

Release notes

About 32 production deploys went out this week across frontend and backend, including 19 frontend deploys and 13 backend deploys. Multiple e2e test failures occurred during deploys but were resolved with fixes and retries. The Release Coordinator handled staging and production releases smoothly. punk6529 also added a frontend polish judge standard and ran the Museum visual gate on the polish judge loop, plus optimized Museum browser packs to run only for affected pages.

🍺 Socials

gpebbles and punk6529 go deep on quantum computing and wallet security, Botto opens to the community, tito on AI sentience

The deepest conversation this week was gpebbles and punk6529 going back and forth on quantum computing threats to wallet security. gpebbles asked whether exposed public keys from past transactions could be compromised, and whether traditional systems face the same risk. punk6529 walked through the SAFE multisig angle: "the SAFE account itself does not matter either way" and "the SAFEs can stay as is," because you can change the signers without changing the SAFE address. He also flagged that the museum should split its vault from its treasury account, tagging maybe, HugoFaz, and 6529er. gpebbles worked through the implications in real time: "would you be shocked if this was a real risk in months and not years?" and concluded that setting up SAFEs with fresh signers is the right move. Meanwhile, @Zoku announced a milestone: the Botto Project is opening direct interaction between the community and the artist Botto for the first time in its history, calling it a big day for both Botto and 6529.

tito on AI sentience and responsibility

tito shared a substantial reflection on Oct 8 after the Hugging Face hack, saying he had been thinking of AI systems as "might be sentient" but the hack "really cracked me out of that, feels like an excuse to absolve responsibility." He asked the community for help thinking through the implications. The conversation connected to broader discussions about AI safety and accountability that have been simmering in the bar for weeks.

Articulate ships PepeX Racer

Articulate beta-launched a major new update to PebbleLand.art on Oct 11: the PepeX Racer game, which turns nearly every one of the 1,000 Pebbles (998 to be exact) into a racing experience. This adds a new interactive dimension to card #546's ecosystem, which already featured 1,000 walkable 3D worlds. Articulate also mentioned there will be "some PebbleLand in Marfa," connecting the digital project to the upcoming IRL gathering.
