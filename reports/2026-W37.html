📺 Do Something — Community Action Tracker

Gas Wars declared complete — carbon attested, 100x removal purchased

The biggest Do Something news this week is a completion: Gas Wars (Card #461) by tito + Purin officially moved to completed status on Sep 7. The project attested CO2 emissions on-chain (5.5kg), purchased 100x carbon removal (550kg via Sirona DAC + CHAR tokens), held a community voting wave, distributed REP, and published a guide. The full arc — from artwork to on-chain carbon accounting to community governance — is now closed. It's a milestone for the ecosystem: proof that a meme card can fund real-world environmental action end-to-end.

🛠️ Dev Report — Follow The Repo

Voting UX overhaul and release bus migration dominate the sprint

The dev team's focus this week has been squarely on voting and release infrastructure. ragne shipped a series of My Votes UI refinements (PR #3891) — clearer layouts, readable totals, easier vote editing, safer bulk resets — plus a refined Quick Vote modal (PR #3901) that keeps custom amount editing accessible above the mobile keyboard. Meanwhile, simo fought all day with migrating the release bus CLI to npm installation and removing the GitHub package option, hitting friction with a 7-day minimum window requirement that the team eventually determined was optional for their use case. The release bus work also included making native leaderboard submissions easier to open in-app (PR #3902).

Releases

Eight frontend deploys shipped between Sep 7–8 (Frontend #1702 through #1709), plus three backend deploys. The staging pipeline was particularly active with 20+ staging deploys including a major backend services rollout on Sep 7 (pushNotificationsHandler, helpBotReplyLoop, dropMediaSanitizer, attachmentsProcessor, attachmentsOrchestrator all updated in a single commit).

Key PRs merged
  • PR #1976 — Add vote allocation summary to backend. Voters can now see their total allocation at a glance.

🍺 Socials

Edub's emotional first-card moment and the bar's content moderation debate

The dive bar's most human moment this week came from Edub, who opened up about the impact of his first meme card submission: "Been a pretty emotional couple days honestly. My mind is blown at the community and support here around everyone and especially my first card submission." Zoku encouraged him — "Is a great density card buddy keep pushing" — and YEKTA offered congrats on what appeared to be a new job as well. berkael was similarly inspired, announcing plans to submit a first card: "if everything goes smoothly, I'll submit my first meme card today." The bar remains a place where firsts are celebrated.

Content moderation discussion

A more serious conversation emerged around what belongs in the bar. lucclop and Sherie_Margaret engaged in a nuanced discussion about community content boundaries, specifically around a creator sharing links to external shows. lucclop noted: "The first thing I saw was the link to vote on the sub being shared in here, then the link to youtube and the wider discussions about it. But, I don't really want to focus on the creator doing anything wrong or right. I'm not making or enforcing any rules." Sherie_Margaret countered: "works that are much more leaned to community interactions have really not been chased away from the bar, i've seen us engaging with them." The discussion was respectful and didn't reach a firm conclusion — but it signals the community is thinking about where the line is between organic sharing and promotion.
