🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

punk6529 ships the Website Studio and a native marketplace

The biggest dev story this week is the Website Studio, announced Sep 12. It's a profile-native CMS builder at /yourhandle/cms/builder that lets anyone build a website on 6529.io. Templates cover artists, collectors, organizations, and The Memes. Publishing works through a wallet-signed message (not an on-chain transaction), and Arweave stores each published version permanently. You can restore or unpublish at any time.

The same day, the full marketplace went live across both The Memes and Meme Lab cards. You can now make offers, buy, and sell directly on 6529.io without going through OpenSea or any third-party marketplace.

Stream Art Documentation (alpha)

Alongside the Website Studio, punk6529 released an alpha of the Stream Art Documentation system. It's a form where artists document their work (provenance, materials, process) for the Network Museum record. The Keys and Gates artists are the first test group. This is the beginning of what will eventually become Stream's documentation layer.

50 PRs merged this week

It was a heavy week for development. 50 PRs merged across the 6529-Collections repos, 21 on Sep 12 and 29 on Sep 13. The major themes:

  • CMS templates: 6 complete templates built with native editing and recovery, plus 18 coordinated color palettes added across all templates.
  • Museum artwork records: Reusable museum artwork records and preservation dossiers, with offline validation standards.
  • Moderation overhaul: Multiple PRs fixing access control, permission recovery, moderation history review, and a developer-only WatchTower for monitoring.
  • Marketplace: Full marketplace interface across Memes and Meme Lab cards, purchase reviews clarified, delivery destinations made editable.
  • Bug fixes: Media resizer bounds, REP aggregation constraints, wave worker alarm noise, CORS compatibility for drop submission, posting outage fix (Sep 13).
  • Meebits #445: Price tracker and animated background restored.
The posting outage

On Sep 13, punk6529 pushed an error reporting update that accidentally broke posting for about 4 hours. He fixed it and posted in the dive bar: "sorry my fault," then "we are back" and a simple "gm." He called it a "network-state provided digital detox." The community took it in stride.

🍺 Socials

The bar finds its voice

Nearly 2,500 drops across seven days. Tuesday alone saw 614 posts. The conversations ranged from punk6529's decentralization push to whether 6529 should show up at the Network State Conference, from a heated debate about sub-shilling fairness to maybe's characteristically unhinged storytelling. A community that's growing, arguing, building, and showing up every single day.

"Our goal is decentralization"

The marketplace going live on 6529.io was the moment it got tangible for people. MAB summed it up: "We've been relieved of having to use OpenSea to buy and sell memes. This was the best change made." meysvm went further: "one of the best things that's happened in 6529 is adding collect and an independent marketplace for 6529 NFTs! HUGE step toward decentralization!" And on Sunday, punk6529 distilled the whole week into one line: "our goal is decentralization. So long as that happens, all the rest will be ok."

Should 6529 show up at the Network State Conference?

SummerMaxi raised it mid-week: the Network State Conference is October 9, and 6529 should be represented. "I can say 6529 is a network society that coordinates public goods work across art, science, culture, and technology and already have granted 4,245 ETH in grants to artists." GhostPepper asked what he thought about the Network School in comparison to what's being built here. maybe took the outside view: "finding people who may care about Freedom, decentralization etc may be the place." The conversation didn't reach a conclusion, but the interest is real.

The shilling debate and the fairness question

lucclop kicked off the week with a pointed observation: "everyone ignored my comments yesterday about shilling subs in the wave and I hate to say it but, seems like its one rule for one and one rule for another on this." blake69 responded by withdrawing his sub to help maybe's Marfa trip, saying he wanted "every full set holder, OG, occasional minter to be happy about the card." By Friday, the bar had turned shilling into a game. MikeKarolos joked "in some parallel universe there is a site called 9256 with a wave called Definitely's dive bar. Today is the only day they don't allow shilling." MadaCollects used her shill to tell everyone to go tell a friend they love them. The bar self-regulated, as it tends to do.

GIANT checks in, the bar shows up

GIANT, one of the most recognized artists in the collection, posted on Thursday: "Sorry bar. been quiet the last few days, I'm going through a really tough time mentally. Love you all." The response was immediate and warm. This is the side of the dive bar that doesn't show up in drop counts. Soliiiart said it well earlier that day: "I really love this bar. Honestly, if I hadn't been active around here, I'm pretty sure my card wouldn't be anywhere near this rank right now."
