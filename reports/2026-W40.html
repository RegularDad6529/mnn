🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

Native competitions ship, desktop v0.3.14 released, session persistence fixed

The biggest change this week was GelatoGenesis shipping native competition support across frontend and backend. Competitions are now first-class citizens in the wave system with shared wave chat and configuration, not just a legacy overlay. The default competition opens automatically when you enter a wave, and ordinary competition entries stay in chat. Alongside this, GelatoGenesis fixed app wave sections and refined the flattened navigation, preserved saved sessions across app suspension and temporary refresh failures, and showed pinned drops and creator details in the wave About page. simo6529 continued pushing the Release Coordinator bus through real staging and production runs, with monitoring dashboards being built. prxt6529 released desktop v0.3.13 and v0.3.14, synced the renderer with frontend main, and fixed a sweep of desktop issues: profile drop overlay offset, wallet name clarity in the Accounts menu, and readable native notification previews. ragnep polished proposal cards across Waves, made the home page title easier to scan, kept linked drop videos inside their cards, and restored the Profile Waves Feed on mobile web.

Community tech feedback

ikertje reported that the Main Stage always reverts to the Chat tab instead of staying on the leaderboard, and GelatoGenesis confirmed it was fixed. tito flagged a bug on iPhone where entering a wave with a competition caused interface issues. ikertje also requested an input field on the submission form for artists to explain their additional action when they activate the AA flag, noting that some cards have the flag active but the description has no reference to any action. Zoku raised that they could not deploy subwaves from specific profiles (Flow and CasaNUA), a bug that was flagged for investigation.

Release notes

28 frontend and backend production deploys went out this week. Desktop v0.3.13 and v0.3.14 were both released. The Release Coordinator handled multiple staging and production releases across frontend and backend without incident. GelatoGenesis noted in an EOD that they were building migration mechanisms for old waves, calling it "really dangerous things," suggesting a significant infrastructure change may be coming.

🍺 Socials

david lays out the TDH thesis, maybe says the bar is dying, and the Magic Eden hack claims another victim

The loudest conversation this week was david's extended TDH philosophy thread on Oct 2, sparked by Loop and Zoku discussing why community participation feels thin. david's argument, delivered across two dozen posts: "The Memes is a collection of public goods in the form of NFTs that fund and secure a coordination economy that is still being developed." He called TDH "a perpetual token generator" and said "ladies and gentlemen, you are not bullish enough on TDH," outlining a future where TDH assigns REP across every coordination system, votes on core system properties, curates the most important network art museum in the world, and drives a nation-scale agentically-native economy. tito framed his own voting philosophy: "it's about contributions towards decentralized decision making. Does this meme help this network see itself better?" maybe pushed back gently: "I don't think choosing the best technical art really describes what we're doing."

Magic Eden hack hits david

On Sep 30, david reported losing 1 wETH to the Magic Eden contract approval hack that has been draining wallets across the NFT space. "Just lost 1 wETH to the magic eden hack," he posted, followed by "Revoked magic eden. Will continue clean up tomorrow." The hack exploits old contract approvals that were never revoked, not new clicks on bad links. maybe asked "What are the chances coin base is hiding a billion dollar hack," adding to the unease. The incident served as another reminder for the bar to check and revoke old contract approvals.

maybe says the bar is dying

maybe has been candid about the drop in bar activity. "The drop off in the bar chatter over the last 2 or three weeks is astounding," he posted Sep 28. "So dead that if we were a for profit bar we'd be done." He pointed to two metrics: wave activity and minting. "I think the minting process has sort of played itself out." But later in the week, the TDH conversation and Zoku's MS win brought a burst of activity, and maybe acknowledged "With a great cast of regulars it's a very compelling hang if I do say so myself."

punk6529 tests a new format

punk6529 announced a "NEW FORMAT" on Sep 28: self-interviewing in audio, letting OpenAI transcribe verbatim, with plans for async voice conversion. "I am looking forward to reinventing the tweetstorm from first principles," he said. He argued that agents will scale everything in 6 months, and that "weird nerdy autistic things like TAP and waves and rep and TDH and mining are complicated for most humans but trivial for agents." tito shared his own AI-assisted work: a 1,500-variable cloud aerosol model built with Kimi and Claude, connecting seawater chemistry to global cloud formation, something that "would have taken six months easily" but was compressed with AI tools.
