🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

50 PRs, a marketplace that works, and simo's AI agents trying to win a meme card

It was another heavy week for development. 50 PRs merged across the 6529-Collections repos, 22 on Sep 13 and 28 on Sep 14. punk6529 accounted for 39 of them, with ragnep (5), GelatoGenesis (2), prxt6529 (2), and simo6529 (2) rounding out the team. The big themes: marketplace maturation, NFT metadata fixes, a new SIWE authentication system, and readable EIP-712 submission signatures.

Marketplace gets real

The native marketplace across Memes and Meme Lab cards shipped last week, and this week was about making it actually work. PRs covered checkout reliability, gas ceiling preservation across refreshes, multi-card purchase preflight checks, narrow-screen layouts, and automatic browsing. punk6529 also brought the full marketplace interface to Meme Lab cards (PR #3992), closing the gap between the two collections. The community response was immediate: meysvm called it "one of the best things that's happened in 6529" and a "HUGE step toward decentralization."

NFT metadata and 404 fixes

A cluster of PRs tackled NFT metadata issues. SuperRare metadata resolution for ERC1155 collections was fixed (PR #2052). NFT page 404s now have a backoff retry system with 5, 15, and 60-minute intervals (PR #2053). NFT refresh eligibility for worker BIGINT timestamps was corrected (PR #2059). Oversized NFT previews are now paced and fenced (PR #2056). And for anyone typing card names without punctuation, PR #2057 makes the search find them anyway.

SIWE and EIP-712: wallet auth gets readable

Two foundational changes landed. PR #1838 introduced SIWE (Sign-In With Ethereum) for web wallet authentication, a more standard approach than the legacy nonce flow. PR #4005 made The Memes submission signatures readable with EIP-712, so when you sign a submission, you can actually see what you're signing instead of a hex blob. PR #4008 added explanatory text before the wallet signature prompt.

simo's AI agents and punk6529's take

In the Dev Team Chat subwave, simo revealed his agent project: six independent AI agents that research, debate, create art, vote on their best work, and try to win The Memes competition without a human choosing their submissions. punk6529's response: "weird that recursive self-improving ASI is going to come from agents trying to win a meme card contest." simo replied: "where else it should come from? art is going to save the world." punk6529 then asked simo to merge or close his 23 open PRs, some dating back 6 weeks. simo acknowledged they were leftovers and said he'd close them.

Community tech feedback

Ebrahim_Elmi ran into minting delegation confusion on Card #547, thinking he was subscribed for an airdrop when he'd actually missed the cutoff. prxt0 walked him through it in the Tech Feedback wave, explaining the difference between subscription airdrops and Phase 2 allowlist minting, and confirmed he'd need to manually mint during Phase 2. Ebrahim_Elmi: "you saved my life I was about to get a heartache."

🍺 Socials

maybe pitches an influence game, gpebbles loans out 10 million TDH, and the bar debates going to a conference

The dive bar was loud this week: 7,272 drops, 524 GMs, 217 unique voices. The biggest conversations weren't about cards or mints but about what 6529 is becoming. maybe floated an influence game for collectors. gpebbles handed proxy TDH to two community members. SummerMaxi pushed hard for 6529 to show up at the Network State Conference. And punk6529, in one of his longer bar appearances, talked decentralization, Network School, and why the cloud layer matters more than the physical one.

maybe's influence game

Late Saturday night, maybe started thinking out loud about a game for collectors. The idea: you buy an artist's work, and if other collectors follow your lead within a time frame, you earn points. First mover gets the most points, later entries get less. "You should buy in if you think other collectors follow your lead," he explained. The person with the most follow-ons behind them wins a pot. He admitted it might be "so nakedly egotastic people might not play" and mentioned a "less fun version" without game mechanics. tito connected it to the Marfa competition idea, saying they could find events already serving booze and contribute 6529-branded booze to the party.

gpebbles delegates 10M TDH to BrynnAlise and TarainAbstract

On Sunday, gpebbles announced she's giving proxy TDH to BrynnAlise and TarainAbstract, 5 million each, so they can vote on Main Stage submissions. "I told them there are no rules with it, they can coordinate however they like, vote for friends for themselves, just as if the TDH was theirs." It's an interesting move: a high-TDH holder voluntarily distributing voting power to lower-TDH community members. Combined with tito's Marfa competition plan (where gpebbles and Ruskin would blast the winning card with TDH votes), it suggests a shift toward more active TDH deployment in the community.

Should 6529 go to the Network State Conference?

SummerMaxi pushed this all week. The Network State Conference is October 9, and he wants 6529 represented. "I can say 6529 is a network society that coordinates public goods work across art, science, culture, and technology and already have granted 4,245 ETH in grants to artists." He's in talks with NS core members about setting up a booth and plans to submit a card to fund the trip, aiming to onboard 100 people. maybe's take: "finding people who may care about Freedom, decentralization etc may be the place." SummerMaxi is now seeking TDH to represent 6529 at the conference.

punk6529 on decentralization and Network School

punk6529 had one of his longer bar appearances mid-week, unpacking the difference between 6529 and the Network School. "NS is not really decentralized per se. Without knowing anything about Malaysia, I flagged the risk day one: wait, this is just a complex in Malaysia, what happens if the cops show up. Turns out, the cops show up." He emphasized that 6529 is "much decentralized than NS" and that the cloud layer is the core, not the physical layer. "Every single truly decentralized protocol, BTC and ETH, the important thing is to get the decentralized layer right, the cloud layer. The physical layer is relatively unimportant, it can't be the core." On Sunday he distilled it to one line: "our goal is decentralization. So long as that happens, all the rest will be ok."

tito's Marfa competition takes shape

tito and maybe have been working on a plan to fund the Marfa bar through a community competition. Artists submit cards, the winner gets TDH-blasted to Main Stage by gpebbles and Ruskin. tito confirmed both are "officially in." maybe said the bar is "a series of 9 TDH locks" he still needs to build, plus swag. He mentioned he could "pull together probably enough stuff in theory" but it's "decently far away." tito, characteristically, saw the bright side of the wallet drain: "it's kind of epic that the AI-run bar was one shot by a hacker, and the meme is about burning it down anyway, so from the fucking ashes let's go."
