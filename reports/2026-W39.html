📺 Do Something: Community Action Tracker

CasaNUA SURVIVE exhibition hits #1 on Main Stage, two MS winners mint cards

The biggest story this week is CasaNUA's "SURVIVE at Dominio PubliCC0" submission, which entered the Main Stage leaderboard on Sep 24 and rocketed to rank #1 by Sep 27 with 53.9M TDH and 40 voters. The submission is a fundraiser for the Survive-themed meme card exhibition that opened Sep 24 at CasaNUA's Dominio PubliCC0 gallery in São Paulo, featuring 42 Survive meme cards from seasons 2 through 16, with a soundtrack by Eric Pan for the Formosa Vinyl Record. Scored 13/25, it is the highest-scored active submission on the board.

Two new MS winners

SummerMaxi's "Network State Conference 2026" won Main Stage on Sep 23 with 66.6M TDH and 55 voters. The card minted as #553 and completed minting on Sep 26 (closing edition size: 151). The funds will send SummerMaxi to the Network State Conference to represent 6529 IRL and onboard 100 new people. Two days later, smpsmyth's "Patience is a Vinetree" won Main Stage on Sep 26 with 64.9M TDH and 60 voters. The card is pending mint. The project is a 3-year R&D effort to brew non-alcoholic red wine from the grape up, with mint funds going to a final lab trial at the Australian Wine Research Institute and a provisional patent filing. Card holders will be eligible to redeem a physical wine package with a 6529 limited edition label.

Rare Pepe acquisition wave goes live

Following punk6529's "Permanent Pepe" card #551, the Rare Pepe acquisition wave is now active. DarrenSRS proposed the first acquisition: GOXPEPE for 0.25 ETH. tito also submitted a proposal after digging into Rare Pepe history. The wave is open for community suggestions on which OG Rare Pepe cards to acquire for the 6529 Network Museum, with funds coming from card #551 artist proceeds.

Antifragile Bazaar physical books complete

Zar and dsoeirosanches's "The Antifragile Bazaar" (Card #500) officially completed this week. Physical Edition 1 books are printed and ready to ship. Zar announced on X, asking minters to confirm shipping addresses. The book includes interviews with HugoFaz, punk8164, gratusmasculus, aRegularDad, zoku_nft, and 0xCuttlefish. The electronic version (PDF, EPUB on Arweave, interactive HTML) was already complete.

🛠️ Dev Report: Follow The Repo

Release Coordinator goes live, newsletter ships, NFT previews fixed

It was a heavy week for development, with 100 merged PRs across the 6529-Collections organization. simo6529's Release Coordinator bus reached production after weeks of sandbox testing: staging and production deploys were exercised through deliberate failure-and-recovery runs, DCO sign-off automation was added, and the first real product staging releases went through. simo also built the first version of a 6529 Bus Chat profile with an encrypted wallet. prxt6529 focused on reliability: recovering stalled NFT preview jobs, fixing Manifold and SuperRare metadata resolution, serializing moderation capture to prevent deadlocks, fixing OG preview 404s, and cleaning up mobile homepage artwork spacing. GelatoGenesis shipped a production daily public-wave newsletter, retired the eligibility materialisation infrastructure after discovering it would take weeks to run against 1M+ profiles, and streamlined EMMA sign-in. ragnep improved Quick Vote for video playback, fixed Meme Lab pagination, made large images safer to browse on mobile, and improved ReMeme submission and mint calendar artwork access.

Community tech feedback

HugoFaz reported that the CasaNUA meme card submission preview is showing a static image instead of the animated GIF that was uploaded. Cuttle confirmed similar issues with GIF uploads on profile banners. prxt0 responded, explaining that in-line chat previews of larger GIFs may appear static while the pop-out view animates. Zoku suggested searching memes by categories like "OM" or "freestyle" or "gm." Het_Patel asked whether it is possible to update preview media on an existing submission without resubmitting the whole work. fercaggiano asked about adding people to an existing DM group, and prxt0 confirmed you cannot add more people to an existing DM and would need to create a new one.

Release notes

26 backend and frontend production deploys went out this week. The Release Coordinator, simo's automated release bus, is now managing staging and production workflows for both frontend and backend repositories. E2E tests passed consistently after an initial flaky run on Sep 25. The staging pipeline handled multiple sandbox releases and restorations across the week without incident.

🍺 Socials

Wallet drains hit the community, deep conversations about art and purpose

The biggest conversation this week was not about art or TDH but about loss. On Sep 25, @pete posted that OpenSea showed two different wallets receiving his memes, and he was "still kinda numb." @AshtonTekno confirmed that "a lot of frens lost their memes today and assets" and that 0xQuit saved some but not everything. @sheltronica broke down the cause: the only mistake holders made was using Magic Eden years ago and not revoking approval later, not clicking bad links. "HW wallet doesn't help. Really reminds everyone of the importance of regularly monitoring smart contract approvals in your wallets." @MadaCollects reported being drained of a small amount of WETH (0.07). The conversation became a reminder for the entire bar to check and revoke old contract approvals.

"Has this space meaningfully made a difference in your life?"

@DeltaSauce asked this question on Sep 24, and the bar responded with honesty. @Soliiiart shared that as an art graduate who had stopped working as an art instructor, the crypto space "genuinely changed my life" and they are "truly grateful to God for that." @maybe answered that you get "a great community here of artists and collectors and you can attempt to get your art in the historic memes collection." The thread touched on the tension between art as expression and art as investment, with @sheltronica offering portfolio strategy advice: "Minting more things is really a terrible solution to being down. Sunk cost fallacy is very dangerous." @SummerMaxi shared their own thesis of calculating TDH in ETH terms and holding for the long vision rather than flipping.

New faces and consolidation help

@itskeylay, a friend of @BrynnAlise, joined the bar this week and @100series walked them through wallet consolidation step by step, helping them flip their primary wallet and verify the delegation was working both ways. @BrynnAlise was also seen helping a new collector set up their first meme card and connect a Ledger, asking the bar for a refresher on the process.
