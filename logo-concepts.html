MNN Logo Concepts

Six variations exploring different ways to connect the letters

#1 Shared Strokes

The right stroke of the first M becomes the left stroke of the second M, which becomes the left stroke of the N. Three letters, one continuous path.

#2 Continuous Zigzag

MNN drawn as a single continuous zigzag line. The peaks and valleys of M flow into the diagonal of N. One stroke, three letters.

#3 Overlapping

Heavy block letters that overlap each other, creating a layered, bold mark. The overlaps create negative space connections.

#4 Rounded Mono

Monospace-style MNN with rounded corners and a shared baseline rail connecting all three letters. Clean, technical, broadcast-feel.

#5 Badge Mark

MNN inside a circular badge with the full name around the edge. Broadcast/news network feel. Works as a favicon and header logo.

#6 Connected Bar

MNN with a horizontal bar connecting the middle of all three letters — like a news ticker line running through the wordmark.

All concepts use the site accent color (#e8a200 amber). Can be refined, combined, or recolored.
